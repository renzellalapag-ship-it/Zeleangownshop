About Zelean

Zelean Gowns, Dresses, Suits and Barong is a family business in Malolos, Bulacan dedicated to helping you look your best for life's most important moments. We offer a curated collection of elegant formalwear — available for rent or purchase — so every occasion can feel special.

Whether you're preparing for a wedding, a debut, a pageant, or a formal celebration, our pieces are chosen for their beauty, fit, and timeless style.

  • Ballgowns, mermaid, long, and night gowns
  • Cocktail dresses for semi-formal occasions
  • Men's suits and the classic Barong Tagalog
  • Filipiniana, bags, and finishing accessories
  • Flexible rent or buy options
